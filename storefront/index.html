Questions

Good to know

How do I pay?

Choose a plan inside AVARA to get a one-time code. Email the code to the AVARA owner at kasula.ashwath3180076@gmail.com and pay by cash or UPI. AVARA unlocks as soon as the owner approves.

Does AVARA work offline?

AVARA checks your plan online and keeps a signed copy of the last answer, so it keeps working offline for up to 7 days (never past your plan’s expiry). Your AI provider may still need internet.

Can I change my plan?

Yes. Before approval, choosing another plan gives you a new code. Later, open your plan screen in AVARA and choose Renew or change plan.

What do I need for the AI features?

A Groq or Gemini API key, or Ollama installed on your computer for local models. Enter keys only in AVARA’s settings, never on this website.

I have a new computer or reinstalled Windows.

A new computer, or a reinstall that deletes AVARA’s data folder, needs a new code. Installing a new version over the old one on the same computer keeps your plan.

Is my data private?

Your chats, settings and files stay on your computer. For your plan, the AVARA server keeps only what it needs: a hashed code, the plan and its dates. See the privacy notice.
