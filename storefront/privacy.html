AVARA Privacy Notice

Draft for review before commercial launch. Configure the actual business contact, processors, retention periods, and account deletion process before taking customer information.

Information collected

The order form requests a name, email address, chosen plan, and account credentials. This draft page does not transmit or store form data.

Use

When connected to production services, account details support sign-in, subscription access, order updates, support, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

Security and sharing

Passwords must be stored using a secure password hashing service. Payment credentials should be handled by the payment provider. Do not place provider secrets in the desktop app or browser code.

Your choices

Provide a way to access, correct, export, and delete account data, subject to legal retention requirements. Contact: business contact to be configured.
