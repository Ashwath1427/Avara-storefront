AVARA Privacy Notice

Draft for review before commercial launch. Configure the actual business contact, processors, retention periods, and account deletion process before taking customer information.

Information collected

The storefront stores the name, email address, password hash, chosen plan, order status, and acceptance record needed to manage the account and fulfill an order. It does not need or request a customer's AI-provider API keys.

Use

Account and order details support sign-in, cash-order review, approval emails, protected software delivery, support, fraud prevention, and legal obligations.

Security and sharing

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Email and hosting providers process the data needed to operate the storefront. Customers must enter AI-provider keys only in AVARA's local settings or approved provider authorization screens, never in this website.

Your choices

Provide a way to access, correct, export, and delete account data, subject to legal retention requirements. Contact: business contact to be configured.
